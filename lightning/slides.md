# You (might) need to change (some of) your habits

---

## Why? you might ask

- cognitive load
- ~speed~ flow
- sake of learning

---

## What to change first?

- focus on what you do most frequently — don't under-estimate micro-interruptions
- my recommendation: copy-paste, launcher, window-manager

# Get to know yourself

## Needs
- what are my frictions
: non-muscle-memory tasks
- what are my distractions
: noise
- what do I forget the most
: hard-to-access information

## Habits
- what am I used to
: keyboard / mouse

## Affordance
- what do I like
: Nice UI / Efficient TUI
- what do I want
: Bundle / DIY

# For the curious: Get to know the tools
- History, ancestors of our tools
- Search "what's an alternative to \<tool>"
- RSS feeds and co.

---

## How to change?

:go step by step

1. Add a new tool
2. Configure existing tools
3. Replace iteratively by using the alternative alongside the rest
4. Use the new flow on a whole work unit
5. Do a cutover

---

## When to change?

- the best time was yesterday, the second best is now
- if you don't have time, make some by using distillation

---

## Share your thoughts

- keep a trace of what you change and why
- Drink coffee with your colleagues about this, they have similar constraints and might have already found the solution
